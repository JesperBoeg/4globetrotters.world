Indonesia (2026)

Home › Destinations › Indonesia

Indonesia was the heart of our 2026 Southeast Asia trip. We started on the island of Flores, based in Labuan Bajo, then boarded a traditional wooden liveaboard for a multi-day voyage through Komodo National Park — Padar sunrises, Komodo dragons, pink beaches, manta rays and whale sharks — all the way across to Lombok. From there we set our sights upward, on the three-day trek up Mount Rinjani, before flying on to the jungles and volcanoes of Sumatra. All our stories from the Indonesia leg are collected below.
